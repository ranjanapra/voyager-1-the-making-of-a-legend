Triaxial Fluxgate Magnetometer (MAG)

NSSDC ID: 77-084A-05
Mission Name: Voyager 1
Principal Investigator: Ness
-------------------
Experiment mass: 5.60 kg
Average experiment power: 2.20 W
Average experiment bit rate: 0.12 kbps
-------------------

Description

This experiment was designed to investigate the magnetic fields of Jupiter
and Saturn, the solar-wind interaction with the magnetospheres of these
planets, and the interplanetary magnetic field out to the solar wind
boundary with the interstellar magnetic field and beyond, if crossed. The
investigation was carried out using two high-field and two low-field
triaxial fluxgate magnetometers. Data accuracy of the interplanetary fields
was plus or minus 0.1 nT, and the range of measurements was from 0.01 nT to
2.E-3 T.
