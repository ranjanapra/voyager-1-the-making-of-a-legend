### 2.1 The One Light-Day Upcoming Projected Milestone
- **Scientific Audit Finding:** The term **ONE LIGHT-DAY** is scientifically verified and mathematically exact:
  $$\text{Distance} = c \times 86{,}400\text{ s} \approx 299{,}792{,}458\text{ m/s} \times 86{,}400\text{ s} \approx 25.902 \times 10^9\text{ km} \approx 173.14\text{ AU}$$
- **Verification Rule:** The website correctly marks this as an **UPCOMING PROJECTED MILESTONE** (~2027) rather than an already achieved event. Voyager 1 is currently at ~165 AU (~22.9 light-hours).
- **Prohibited Term Avoidance:** The term "one light-year" (~63,241 AU) is strictly prohibited and completely absent from the codebase.

### 2.2 Heliopause Crossing & Interstellar Space
- **Scientific Fact:** Voyager 1 crossed the heliopause on **August 25, 2012** at a heliocentric distance of approximately **121.6 AU** (~18.2 billion km).
- **Verification Rule:** The website accurately explains that crossing into the interstellar medium does not mean exiting the gravitational boundary of the Solar System; the vast Oort Cloud extends from ~2,000 to ~100,000 AU and will take Voyager 1 another ~300 years to reach and ~30,000 years to traverse.
