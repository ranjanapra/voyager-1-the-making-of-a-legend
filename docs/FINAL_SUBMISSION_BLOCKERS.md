## 2. THE SILENT HARDWARE & INTERSTELLAR HERITAGE

*Voyager 1 — The Making of a Legend* explores the poignant reality of deep-space equipment triage:
- To allow Voyager 1 to survive in interstellar space and cross the heliopause at 121 AU, NASA flight controllers were forced to systematically turn off seven vital scientific instruments (the cameras, infrared and ultraviolet spectrometers, photopolarimeter, and planetary radio astronomy).
- By turning these instruments into "silent hardware", the mission conserved **131.0 Watts** of scarce electrical power, allowing the probe's magnetic field and plasma wave instruments to keep operating into the 2030s.
- The dedicated Silent Hardware Archive view (Explore Tab 04) honors the complete 11-instrument suite, ending with the operating Plasma Wave Subsystem (PWS).
